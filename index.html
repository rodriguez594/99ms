Supplements · tillæg

AI scanning

Scanning receipts uses Claude from Anthropic with your own API key. Make one at console.anthropic.com under API keys. The key is saved only on this device and is only sent to Anthropic. A scan costs about 20 øre.

Help

The setup questions walk you through pay, payday, fixed shifts, supplements and a savings goal. Running them again only adds what is new.

Backup

Export a backup now and then, and import it on a new phone or after clearing your browser. When you are logged in, your data also syncs to your account.

Start over

Erases your shifts, savings, goals, calendar and settings on every device you are logged in on, then asks the setup questions again. Export a backup first if you might want anything back.

    Shift without the calendar

    Only for a simple daily shift. If you use the Calendar, leave this empty: the clock follows the calendar.

    Shifts over 4.5 hours include an unpaid 30-minute break. Hourly pay assumes a 37-hour week when shown per month or year.

    Save shift

    This shift is still going. Its pay goes into savings live, every millisecond, until the shift ends. Tap End shift on the clock when you clock out.
