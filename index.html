Salary & shift

Per

With a shift set, the clock counts your pay for working hours only (a 37-hour week). The end time is optional. Shifts over 4.5 hours include an unpaid 30-minute break, and the clock pauses during it. Leave the break time empty and it is placed for you: mid-shift, and never later than 4 hours in. Leave the shift start empty to count around the clock instead.

Save shift

This shift is still going. Its pay goes into savings live, every millisecond, until the shift ends. Tap End shift on the clock when you clock out.
