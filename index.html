Salary & shift

Per

Pay period: for example from day 19 means the 19th to the 18th of the next month. Payday is the day of the month the period is paid out, after it ends.

With a shift set, the clock counts your pay for working hours only (a 37-hour week). The end time is optional. Shifts over 4.5 hours include an unpaid 30-minute break, and the clock pauses during it. Leave the break time empty and it is placed for you: mid-shift, and never later than 4 hours in. Leave the shift start empty to count around the clock instead. For shifts on different days, use Calendar: once it has shifts, the clock follows it.

Save shift

This shift is still going. Its pay goes into savings live, every millisecond, until the shift ends. Tap End shift on the clock when you clock out.
